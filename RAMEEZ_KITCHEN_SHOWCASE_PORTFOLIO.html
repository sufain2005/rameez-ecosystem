📱 The Four System Pillars
Engineered with specialized workflows for each participant in the delivery lifecycle.

1. Customer Mobile App Flutter Web/Mobile

Frictionless food ordering, coupons, VIP membership, and live order tracking.

  • Dynamic Food Catalog: Charcoal Dum Biryanis, Tandoori Platters, Rolls with live availability badges.
  • Rameez Gold VIP Pass: Monthly VIP tier with ₹0 packaging & free delivery across all orders.
  • Multi-Channel Checkout: 1-Tap Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Rameez Digital E-Wallet, and COD.
  • 8-Step Live Order Pipeline: Real-time animated tracker with assigned driver name, phone, vehicle, and live ETA.
  • Instant Ratings & Tips: Post-delivery star feedback and tip incentives directly credited to drivers.

2. Merchant Command Center Kitchen Operations

Real-time order triage, kitchen preparation states, driver dispatch, and master vault.

  • Dual-Branch Operations: Flagship 60 Feet Ring Road & Express Hub Nittuvalli Circle.
  • Live Kitchen Pipeline: Status transitions (Pending ➔ Preparing / Cooking ➔ Ready for Pickup).
  • Auto Driver Dispatch: Auto-triggers telephone alarm to online drivers with 30-second round-robin cascade.
  • Executive CD Vault: Confidential Customer & Driver Master Directory secured with reverse admin passkey.
  • Permanent Driver Deletion: Admin control to permanently remove drivers with complete relational cascade.

3. Driver Logistics App Fleet Navigation

Turn-by-turn delivery navigation, order alarms, earnings ledger, and tip feed.

  • Online / Offline Duty Switch: High-visibility toggle controlling kitchen dispatch eligibility.
  • 30s Countdown Incoming Alarm: High-priority order accept modal with timer and restaurant pickup badge.
  • 1-Tap Google Maps Driving Navigation: Direct driving directions to restaurant & customer drop-off without GPS permission roadblocks.
  • Real-Time GPS Beacon: Broadcasts live coordinates to customer room for millisecond-level map updates.
  • Customer Feedback Feed: Live view of customer ratings, positive tags, and earned doorstep tips.

4. Real-Time Cloud Engine Node.js + PostgreSQL

High-throughput WebSockets hub, relational ledger, and security auditor.

  • Socket.IO Room Multiplexing: Targeted order:{orderId} rooms preventing cross-tenant telemetry leaks.
  • PostgreSQL Relational Ledger: Normalised schemas for drivers, sessions, deliveries, earnings, and OTPs.
  • Auto-Reoffer Worker: Background job re-offering unaccepted deliveries to available online fleet.
  • Security Audit Logging: Automatic security logs for logins, password changes, driver deletions, and membership upgrades.
  • Rate Limiting & Helmet: OWASP-compliant headers, DDOS shielding, and bcrypt password hashing.

⚡ Key Architectural Innovations
Engineered for real-world resilience, low latency, and maximum user delight.
🚀

30s Cascade Dispatch

Zero Delay

Continuous round-robin dispatch ensures no customer order gets stuck. If a driver does not accept within 30 seconds, the engine automatically notifies the next online driver and warns the merchant if all drivers are busy.

🛡️

Executive CD Vault

Reverse Auth

Confidential driver and customer data is protected inside an encrypted Executive CD Vault. Unlocking requires a reverse-passkey algorithm hidden from frontend exposure, with every access attempt logged to an immutable security audit trail.

🗺️

1-Tap Google Maps

Frictionless GPS

Drivers launch native Google Maps turn-by-turn driving navigation directly from the active order card for both restaurant pickup and customer doorstep, leaving origin dynamic so GPS locks instantly without permission friction.

💾 Relational Database Architecture (PostgreSQL)
Normalized tables with full cascade protection and high-performance foreign key indexes.
Table Name Primary Keys / Identity Foreign Keys & References Purpose / Features
drivers driver_id (SERIAL), driver_code Unique Phone & Email Fleet profile, vehicle details, rating, active status, bcrypt password hash.
driver_deliveries delivery_id (SERIAL) driver_id ➔ drivers(driver_id) Active & historical delivery jobs, order ID, customer details, drop-off coordinates, status.
delivery_offers offer_id (SERIAL) driver_id, delivery_id 30-second ephemeral dispatch offers, cascade expiration timestamps, accept/decline state.
driver_earnings earning_id (SERIAL) driver_id, delivery_id Financial ledger recording base earnings, customer tips, bonus incentives, and payouts.
driver_sessions session_id (UUID) driver_id ➔ drivers(driver_id) Multi-device login tokens, IP audit logs, device fingerprint, and instant remote revocation.
