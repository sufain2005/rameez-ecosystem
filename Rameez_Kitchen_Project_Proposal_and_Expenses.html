🔄 2. Step-by-Step Order Journey (How It Works)
1
Customer Orders
Selects Chicken Dum Biryani, enters address via GPS, applies coupon, and confirms with Google Pay or Cash.
2
Kitchen Accepts
Kitchen tablet rings loudly. Manager taps "Accept" and changes status to "Cooking". Customer app updates live.
3
Food Ready
When packed, kitchen clicks "Ready for Pickup". Central cloud immediately searches for the nearest online driver.
4
30s Driver Alert
Driver phone rings loudly with a 30-second countdown. Driver taps "ACCEPT". (If missed, it rings the next online driver).
5
Pickup & Delivery
Driver follows 1-tap Google Maps directions to restaurant, picks up food parcel, and drives to customer doorstep.
6
Secret OTP Handover
Customer tells driver their 4-digit secret OTP to confirm handover. Customer leaves 5-star rating & driver tip.
🛡️ 3. Built-In Security & Anti-Hacking Protection

To prevent tech-savvy users or hackers from using intercept tools (like Burp Suite or Modded APKs) to alter food prices or steal customer records, we implemented bank-grade protections:

Threat / Attack What Hackers Try to Do How Rameez Kitchen Protects You
Price Tampering Changing Chicken Biryani price from ₹250 to ₹1 in transit. HMAC-SHA256 Digital Signatures: Backend immediately drops modified orders with 403 Forbidden.
Replay Attacks Resending the same payment or free coupon code 100 times. One-Time Nonce Cache: Repeated requests are automatically blocked by the server.
Source Code Theft Decompiling APK to steal business logic or secret keys. AOT Obfuscation & Symbol Stripping: Code is converted to scrambled machine code (R8/ProGuard).
